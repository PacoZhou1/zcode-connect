## What is it?

Your AI task is still running on your computer, but you need to head out.

Zcode Connect lets you connect to ZCode on your computer from an iPhone or iPad. Check progress, continue a conversation, and review file changes. Your computer keeps running the task; your phone gives you a way to stay involved.

It is an independently developed native iOS client with a Liquid Glass interface, not an official Z.ai product.

## What you can do

- **Pick up existing work**: open projects and tasks, continue conversations, and read replies and execution steps.
- **Switch computers**: save connections to multiple computers and choose the one you need.
- **Follow your agents**: view sub-agent progress and conversations as a task moves forward.
- **Review files and changes**: browse project files and inspect changes made during a task.
- **Keep your model setup**: available models and options come from the connected computer.
- **Choose your connection**: use ZCode's official relay, or configure your own relay if you have hosting experience.

The App supports Simplified Chinese, English, Japanese, French, Korean, and German. It follows your system language by default, with a separate language choice in Settings.

## Get started

1. Install the App from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6820455082). Requires **iOS / iPadOS 26 or later**.
2. Set up [ZCode](https://zcode.z.ai/) on your computer. Use a desktop version that supports remote connections and open its remote connection entry.
3. Scan the pairing code or paste the complete pairing information on your phone. Check the computer and relay address, then confirm.
4. Choose a project, open a task, and carry on.

**Your computer must stay awake and online, with ZCode running.** The App is free; your chosen model providers or third-party services may charge separately. Keep pairing codes and links private: they contain connection credentials.

You do not need your own server to use the official relay. Self-hosting is an advanced option that requires a separately configured server and desktop. Cloning this repository does not set those up for you.
